"The Accessibility Gap"

A single trip on a loose rug often marks the end of independence. We believe aging isn't a disease, and your home shouldn't be a hazard.

Why Our Homes Aren't Ready for Us

By 2030, 71 million Americans will be over 65.

However, a staggering 96.5% of our current houses aren't physically prepared. Most homes were designed for young, able-bodied families. For a senior, narrow doorways and high-walled tubs aren't just design flaws—they're obstacles to living with dignity.

When we talk about 'The Home as Medicine,' we're talking about a mindset shift. We aren't just renovating rooms; we're preventing the falls that break families apart.

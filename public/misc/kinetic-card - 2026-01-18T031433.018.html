In Central Texas, a home is more than four walls and a roof. It’s the notches on the doorframe marking a grandchild’s height; it’s the smell of a kitchen that has seen fifty years of Sunday dinners; it’s the peace of a front porch at sunset.

The Tragedy of the System

For most seniors in the Killeen-Temple-Belton area, the greatest fear isn’t aging—it’s the "Facility." It’s the moment their independence is traded for a sterile hallway and a meal schedule they didn’t choose. When an elder is forced out of their home, they don't just move; they begin to fade.

The HOMEase Obsession

At HOMEase, we believe that "aging in place" shouldn't be a luxury—it should be a human right. We aren’t just a staffing agency that sends nurses to check boxes. We are Home Preservators.

We’ve married the soul of traditional caregiving with the power of proprietary technology. While others see a "patient," we see a legacy that needs protecting. Our technology doesn't just "assess" a home; it maps out a blueprint for safety so that a grandmother never has to fear her own bathroom, and a son never has to wake up in a cold sweat wondering if his father fell.

Our Mission

Founded by a Native American entrepreneur with a deep-rooted cultural reverence for our elders, HOMEase is on a mission to ensure that every senior in Central Texas can keep their front porch.

"We provide skilled nursing and therapy, yes. But what we actually deliver is Dignity."

We provide the medical expertise of a hospital with the heart of a neighbor. From palliative care that honors the spirit to rehabilitation that restores the walk, we are here to ensure that the "Golden Years" are spent where they belong: at home.

The HOMEase Standard

We don't measure success by "episodes of care." We measure it by the number of nights our clients sleep soundly in their own beds, and the number of families who can finally stop being "care managers" and go back to being sons and daughters.

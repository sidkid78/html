The R-F-D Architectural Philosophy

Complexity is the enemy of stability. Our system utilizes a Retain-Focus-Delete (R-F-D) orchestration to prevent "AI Drift":

01. Node A

Retain

A high-level Orchestrator (Gemini 3 Pro) holds the vision of the perfect environment.

02. Node B

Focus

Specialized Worker Agents (Gemini 3 Flash) execute surgical tasks with extreme speed and zero context-bloat.

03. Node C

Delete

To ensure security and total isolation, every worker is purged the moment the job is done. No residue. No ghosts in the machine.

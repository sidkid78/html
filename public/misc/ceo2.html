Clifton Franco

Building Certainty in an Uncertain Market

Most people see a residential development as a collection of blueprints and bottom lines. Clifton Franco sees the heartbeat of Central Texas.

In an industry defined by volatility, Clifton has spent over 15 years acting as the bridge between a builder’s vision and a family’s future.

He doesn't just "manage sales teams"—he architects the human experience required to turn a construction site into a neighborhood.

While others focus on the transaction, Clifton is obsessed with the transition.

He understands that for a builder, a "slow launch" is a threat to the legacy; for a buyer, a "bad process" is a threat to their peace of mind.

By merging high-level strategic marketing with a deep, intuitive grasp of buyer psychology, Clifton has consistently turned "underperforming" into "unprecedented."

From the boardroom of the Home Builders Association to the muddy boots of a new community launch, his mission remains the same: To eliminate the friction of the home-buying process.

Whether he is hitting 125% of a closing goal or mentoring the next generation of sales leaders, Clifton isn't just selling square footage. He is building the economic and emotional foundations that Central Texas stands on.

He doesn’t just close deals;
he opens doors.

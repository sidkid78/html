The Clinical Gap

The Challenge of Aging in Place

The challenge of aging in place is not merely a logistical hurdle for families; it is a critical clinical gap that often results in preventable hospital readmissions and a decline in patient autonomy.

For the HOMEase pilot expansion, healthcare professional outreach serves as the primary pillar of growth. We are moving beyond direct-to-consumer marketing to engage trusted advisors: Occupational Therapists (OTs), Physical Therapists (PTs), and Home Health Agencies (HHAs).

Force Multiplier

These professionals are the frontline defense against the "revolving door" of geriatric care. By providing AI-assisted safety reports that validate professional expertise, HOMEase acts as a clinical force multiplier. This ensures every safety recommendation is backed by objective data.

Targeting Occupational and Physical Therapists

Occupational and Physical Therapists are the most critical high-leverage targets. Home safety evaluations are fundamental to their practice, but manual processes are subjective and time-consuming.

HOMEase transforms this workflow by offering AI-assisted safety reports that make clinical documentation faster, more consistent, and defensible. We target independent OTs, rehab clinics, and outpatient groups using data from AOTA and TOTA.

The Pilot Pitch: Therapists

  • Professional Empowerment: Free, branded PDF reports for discharge notes.
  • Liability Mitigation: Technology carries no increase in professional liability.
  • Early Influence: Opportunity to shape product features for clinical rigor.

Engaging Home Health Agencies

Medicare Incentives & Outcomes

HHAs operate in a high-stakes environment where reimbursement is tied to outcomes. HOMEase provides data to prove success. We target Medicare-certified HHAs and providers accepting VA community care referrals, aiding them in meeting CMS Quality Reporting requirements.

Pilot Pitch: HHAs

Compliance Support Data-driven documentation for CMS requirements.

Collaboration with Caregiver and Aging Organizations

Caregiver and aging organizations (AAA, senior services, churches) are the gatekeepers to families. Our outreach focuses on a "peace-of-mind" value proposition. For the adult child miles away, the platform offers a transparent look at home safety without the intrusion of traditional inspection.

SEAMLESS LOOP

Strategic Integration & OT-Contractor Pairing

To ensure sustainability, we introduce the OT-Contractor Pairing Model. An OT performs the assessment using HOMEase, and a pre-qualified contractor receives the referral. HOMEase serves as the neutral system of record, eliminating communication breakdowns.

We are also initiating hospital discharge partnerships on a micro-scale—"discharge safety add-ons"—allowing us to demonstrate value without complex enterprise integration.

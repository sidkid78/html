The Danger

The most dangerous place for an American over the age of 65 isn't a highway or a crowded city—it is their own bathroom.

Every 11 seconds, an older adult is treated in an emergency room for a fall. Every 19 minutes, one of those falls is fatal. But the tragedy isn't just the injury; it’s the aftermath. A single trip on a loose rug or a slip in a tub often marks the end of independence, forcing seniors out of the homes they love and into the sterile, expensive, and lonely world of institutional care.

We call this the "Accessibility Gap," but for families, it feels like a ticking clock.

The Impact

2030

By 2030, 71 million Americans will hit the age of 65. Only 3.5% of our housing stock is ready for them. This is a massive structural failure, but it is also our greatest opportunity to do good.

By streamlining the path from "unsafe" to "accessible," we aren't just generating revenue; we are preventing the falls that break families apart. We are turning the $161 billion aging-in-place market into a movement for human dignity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just for people with a lot of money? ▼
No. We are actively partnering with federal grant programs (like HUD’s OAHMP) to ensure that our assessment tools and contractor network are available to low-income seniors who need these modifications the most.
Do I need to be a tech expert to use the app? ▼
If you can take a photo, you can use HOMEase. We’ve designed the interface specifically for the "Adult Child" and their parents, focusing on large icons, clear instructions, and zero technical friction.
How do you vet your contractors? ▼
We don't just check licenses. We prioritize contractors who hold "Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist" (CAPS) designations. These are professionals who understand the intersection of construction and clinical safety.
What happens to my data? ▼
Your home is your sanctuary. We use the highest levels of encryption and adhere to strict privacy standards. Your spatial data is used only to generate your safety report and connect you with a qualified professional.
Is this an emergency response system? ▼
No. HOMEase is proactive, not reactive. We want to fix the home *before* the emergency happens. We are the "Pre-Fall" solution.

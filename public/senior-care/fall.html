INTRODUCTION // PUBLIC HEALTH CHALLENGE

The United States is currently navigating a profound public health challenge. This crisis is unfolding not within the sterile corridors of hospitals, but within the private walls of our homes. By 2030, the population of adults aged 65 and older is projected to swell to over 71 million, marking an unprecedented demographic shift.

The overwhelming majority of these individuals—between 75% and 87%—express a deep-seated desire to 'age in place,' maintaining their independence within their own communities. However, this aspiration is colliding with a harsh structural reality: our national housing stock is fundamentally ill-equipped for an aging population.

LIMITATIONS OF TRADITIONAL ASSESSMENTS

For decades, the gold standard has been in-person assessment by OTs. However, manual approaches face inherent challenges:


  • Subjectivity: Manual measurements vary between assessors.
  • Time Intensity: Manual auditing is a bottleneck.
  • Visualization Gap: Homeowners struggle to visualize abstract recommendations.
  • Fragmented Workflow: Handoff errors create new hazards.
